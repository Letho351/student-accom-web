About StudentAccomo

StudentAccomo is a student-focused accommodation platform built to help Vaal University of Technology (VUT) students find safe, affordable, and verified housing near campus.

Our mission is to simplify the accommodation search process by connecting students with trusted landlords, allowing them to browse, compare, and inquire effortlessly. Whether you are a first-year student or returning, StudentAccomo ensures a smooth and stress-free experience.
